    I · Fundamentals

    握拍：沒有標準答案The grip has no single right answer

    張教練的第一課很務實：握法只是參考，能把球穩定處理好才是重點。初學者先用東方式打底，想加上旋時再換西方式；而握得多緊，則要看你正在打哪一顆球。Coach Chang's first lesson is practical: grips are a reference point, and handling the ball reliably is what counts. Beginners build on the Eastern grip, switch toward Western when they want topspin—and how hard you squeeze depends on which shot you're hitting.

    握拍沒有一定標準，能將球處理好最重要。There's no fixed standard for the grip. Handling the ball well is what matters most.張教練 · 教學 33〈球拍該怎麼握〉Coach Chang · Lesson 33, How to hold the paddle (translated)

    II · Serve & Return

    開局六招：三發三接Six ways to start a point: three serves, three returns

    張教練用「時鐘」描述拍面角度與擊球點：5-5 拍面約仰角 45°，8-2 拍面微朝上；擊球點的幾點鐘則是球的位置。Coach Chang describes paddle face and contact with a clock: a 5-5 face is open about 45°, an 8-2 face is slightly open; the "o'clock" contact point refers to the position on the ball.

    III · Net Play

    截擊：先求穩，再求狠Volleys: steady first, then sharp

    初學者先練 8-2 拍面帶切，把球擋穩；進階者再加入平擊與帶上旋截擊，提升攻擊力。帶上旋的仰角要依來球高度調整，8-2 只是參考。Beginners start with the 8-2 slice volley to block steadily; advanced players add the flat and topspin volley for more attack. On the topspin volley, adjust the face to the ball's height—8-2 is just a reference.

    IV · Kitchen Control

    廚房區：耐心與突破The kitchen: patience and the breakthrough

    丁克對峙的第一原則是穩定、不亂失誤；但耐心只是手段——等到對手送出偏高的球，你要有三種方法把局面打開。Rule one of the dink rally: be steady, no free errors. But patience is a means, not the goal—when the ball pops up, you need three ways to break the rally open.

    丁克 · 教學 11Dinks · Lesson 11
    大哉問The big question

    什麼時候該停止丁克、開始進攻？When do you stop dinking and attack?

    張教練的答案是：等對手回球過高。下面三招都建立在同一個前提上——先用丁克把對手逼出一顆「可以打」的球，再決定是突襲、挑高，還是斜角上旋。Coach Chang's answer: when the opponent's ball comes up too high. All three plays below share that premise—use the dink to force an attackable ball, then choose the speed-up, the lob or the inside-out topspin.

    突破丁克 · 教學 17Breaking the dink · Lesson 17
    V · Power

    強抽、扣殺、重推：終結三連發Drive, smash, push: the finishing trio

    三招的共同點：以小臂發力為主。差別在於來球高度——肩膀以上扣殺、偏高但在肩下用重推、中場則用帶上旋的強抽把球壓進底線。What they share: the forearm does most of the work. What separates them is ball height—smash above the shoulder, hard-push a high ball below the shoulder, and drive from mid-court with topspin to keep it in.
