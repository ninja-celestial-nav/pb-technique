    I · Rules & Scoring

    上場之前：規則與計分Before you play: rules and scoring

    技術練得再好，也得先懂規則：哪裡算界內、什麼時候不能截擊、比分怎麼報。本章依 USA Pickleball 2026 年官方規則，按一分球的順序整理：先認識球場，再看發球、雙彈跳、廚房與界線怎麼判，最後學會計分、報分與站位。Great technique still needs the rules behind it: what's in, when you can't volley, how to call the score. Following USA Pickleball's 2026 rules, this chapter follows the order of a point: the court first, then how the serve, two-bounce rule, kitchen and lines are judged, and finally how to score, call it and stand.

    A · 場地與擊球規則A · The court and the rules of play
    01

    球場The court

    Court
    • 20 × 44 英尺（約 6.10 × 13.41 公尺），單雙打同尺寸20 × 44 ft (6.10 × 13.41 m), same for singles and doubles
    • 網高：兩側 36 英寸（91.44 公分），中央 34 英寸（86.36 公分）Net: 36 in at the sidelines, 34 in at the center
    02

    發球The serve

    Serve
    • 一般發球（截擊式發球）：手臂由下往上揮、擊球點不高於腰、拍頭不高於手腕最高點Volley serve: upward arm arc, contact below the waist, paddle head not above the wrist
    • 落地發球：讓球從手中或拍面自然落下，彈地後再擊；不可往下丟或往上拋，不受上一條三項限制Drop serve: let the ball fall naturally from your hand or paddle and hit it after the bounce—no throwing down or tossing up; the three volley-serve limits don’t apply
    • 至少一腳在底線後，不可踩線或超出中線、邊線延長線At least one foot behind the baseline; don't touch the line or cross the center/side extensions
    • 對角發球，每人只有一次機會Serve diagonally; one attempt per server
    03

    雙彈跳規則Two-bounce rule

    Two-bounce rule
    • 接發方必須讓發球落地彈起再回擊The receivers must let the serve bounce
    • 發球方也必須讓接發球落地彈起再回擊The servers must then let the return bounce
    • 兩次彈跳之後，雙方才可截擊After both bounces, either side may volley
    04

    廚房（非截擊區）The kitchen (NVZ)

    Non-volley zone
    • 網前兩側各 7 英尺（約 2.13 公尺），含線7 feet from the net on each side, lines included
    • 在區內或踩線時截擊＝失誤Volleying while in it or on its line is a fault
    • 截擊後慣性帶進區內也算失誤，即使球已經死球Momentum carrying you in after a volley is a fault, even if the ball is already dead
    • 不截擊時，隨時可以進廚房You may be in the kitchen anytime you're not volleying
    05

    壓線Lines

    Line calls
    • 球碰到任何線都算界內A ball touching any line is in
    • 唯一例外：發球落在廚房線上＝短球失誤One exception: a serve on the kitchen line is short—a fault
    06

    失誤Faults

    Faults
    • 出界、掛網、兩次落地才回擊Out, into the net, or letting it bounce twice
    • 雙彈跳前截擊、在廚房內截擊Volleying before both bounces, or from the kitchen
    • 死球前人、衣物或球拍碰網或網柱；球打到身上You, your clothing or paddle touching the net or post during play; the ball hitting you
    • 接發方失誤＝發球方得分；發球方失誤＝換發Receiving fault = point to servers; serving fault = loss of serve
    發球擦網、落在正確發球區？繼續打。發球沒有「重發」。Serve clips the net and lands in the right box? Play on. There are no lets on serves.最常見的誤會 · 本刊整理The most common myth · Our note
    B · 計分、報分與站位B · Scoring, calling and positions
    07

    只有發球方得分Only the serving side scores

    Side-out scoring
    • 傳統計分（Side-out）只有發球方能得分In traditional side-out scoring, only the serving team scores
    • 接發方贏球不加分，只拿回發球權Win a rally while receiving and you get the serve, not a point
    • 一般打到 11 分、須領先 2 分；比賽也可能打 15 或 21 分Games go to 11, win by 2; tournaments may play to 15 or 21
    08

    雙打報三個數字Doubles calls three numbers

    Score call
    • 順序：發球方分數 → 接發方分數 → 發球員號碼（1 或 2）Order: serving score → receiving score → server number (1 or 2)
    • 每次拿回發球權，由站在右區的球員擔任 1 號發球After each side-out, the player in the right-hand court serves first as server 1
    • 單打只報兩個數字：發球方、接發方Singles calls just two numbers: server, then receiver
    09

    開局 0-0-2 與站位The 0-0-2 start and positions

    Start of game · positions
    • 開局第一輪只有一位發球員，失誤就直接換對方發球The first serving turn of a game gets only one server; a fault is an immediate side-out
    • 發球方得分後，發球員與搭檔換邊，同一人繼續發When the serving team scores, the server switches courts with their partner and serves again
    • 站位口訣：開局第一位發球員，自家分數偶數站右、奇數站左Position check: the game's first server is on the right when their team's score is even, left when odd

    C · 2026 規則更新C · What changed in 2026

    落球得分（Rally scoring）取消「凍結」Rally scoring drops the "freeze"

    落球得分制是每一球都計分，不論誰發球。2025 年的版本在賽末點規定只有發球方能得分；2026 年規則 14.A.2 取消了這個「凍結」，接發方也能拿下致勝分。傳統 Side-out 計分仍是 USA Pickleball 的預設制度，落球得分屬於可選用的賽制。In rally scoring, every rally earns a point, whoever served. The 2025 version said only the serving side could win game point; 2026 Rule 14.A.2 removed that freeze, so the receiving side can win the final point too. Traditional side-out scoring remains USA Pickleball's default; rally scoring is an optional format.

    依據：USA Pickleball 2026 官方規則，經 2026 規則變更文件（Rule 14.A.2）、USA Pickleball 發球說明及多家規則整理網站交叉查證（2026/11/17）。本頁為摘要，正式比賽以官方規則書與賽事規程為準。Based on the USA Pickleball 2026 rules, cross-checked against the 2026 Rulebook Change Document (Rule 14.A.2), USA Pickleball’s serving guide and several rules explainers (17 Nov 2026).     II · Fundamentals

    握拍：沒有標準答案The grip has no single right answer

    張教練的第一課很務實：握法只是參考，能把球穩定處理好才是重點。初學者先用東方式打底，想加上旋時再換西方式；而握得多緊，則要看你正在打哪一顆球。Coach Chang's first lesson is practical: grips are a reference point, and handling the ball reliably is what counts. Beginners build on the Eastern grip, switch toward Western when they want topspin—and how hard you squeeze depends on which shot you're hitting.

    IV · Net Play

    截擊：先求穩，再求狠Volleys: steady first, then sharp

    初學者先練 8-2 拍面帶切，把球擋穩；進階者再加入平擊與帶上旋截擊，提升攻擊力。帶上旋的仰角要依來球高度調整，8-2 只是參考。Beginners start with the 8-2 slice volley to block steadily; advanced players add the flat and topspin volley for more attack. On the topspin volley, adjust the face to the ball's height—8-2 is just a reference.

    V · Kitchen Control

    廚房區：耐心與突破The kitchen: patience and the breakthrough

    丁克對峙的第一原則是穩定、不亂失誤；但耐心只是手段——等到對手送出偏高的球，你要有三種方法把局面打開。Rule one of the dink rally: be steady, no free errors. But patience is a means, not the goal—when the ball pops up, you need three ways to break the rally open.

    大哉問The big question

    什麼時候該停止丁克、開始進攻？When do you stop dinking and attack?

    張教練的答案是：保持耐心，等對手回球過高再搶攻。下面是他示範的三種突破方式，各有出手條件——突襲要在球微高的瞬間；挑高球要高過對手能扣殺的高度；Inside-Out 上旋斜角則要球速夠快。Coach Chang's answer: stay patient and attack when their ball comes up too high. Below are the three breakthroughs he demonstrates, each with its own condition—speed up the instant the ball sits up; lob only above their smash reach; hit the inside-out topspin fast.

    VI · Power

    強抽、扣殺、重推：終結三連發Drive, smash, push: the finishing trio

    三招都是終結球，差別在來球高度與位置：肩膀以上可以扣殺；偏高但在肩下、無法扣殺時用重推；中場則用帶上旋的強抽把球壓進底線。強抽與重推以小臂發力為主；扣殺初學先別用手腕，熟練後再加。All three finish points; ball height and position decide which: smash above the shoulder, hard-push a high ball that’s still below it, and drive from mid-court with topspin to keep it in. The drive and push are forearm-led; on the smash, leave the wrist out until the motion is grooved.
