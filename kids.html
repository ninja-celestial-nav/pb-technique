第零關 · 上場之前Level 0 · Before you play

安全與運動精神Safety and good sportsmanship

先熱身、保護好自己，再公平地玩。Warm up, stay safe, play fair.
保護自己Stay safe
①先熱身：慢跑一下，轉轉手腕、肩膀和腳踝，再開始打球。Warm up: jog a little and circle your wrists, shoulders and ankles before you play.
②記得喝水，穿運動鞋（不要穿拖鞋）。有護目鏡更好。Drink water and wear sports shoes (no slippers). Eye protection is even better.
③往後退的時候側身移動，不要倒退跑，比較不容易跌倒。Move sideways when you go back—don't run backwards—so you're less likely to fall.
公平比賽Play fair
④有別的球滾進場：大聲喊「有球！」，大家停下來，這一球重打。不要踩到球！A ball rolls onto your court? Shout "Ball!", everyone stops, and the point is replayed. Never step on a ball!
⑤誠實判球：自己這一邊的球自己判。碰到線算界內；看不清楚，就算界內。Call it honestly: you call the balls on your side. Touching the line is in—and if you're not sure, it's in.
⑥比賽結束，到網前和大家碰拍或握手，說聲「謝謝」。After the game, meet at the net, tap paddles or shake hands, and say "thank you."

「有球滾進場可以重打」和「看不清楚算界內」是 USA Pickleball 的規則；其他是本刊建議的好習慣。"Replay when a ball rolls on" and "if in doubt, it's in" come from USA Pickleball's rules; the rest are good habits we recommend.

小挑戰Challenge
上場前帶大家一起做 5 分鐘熱身；比賽結束後，和每一個人碰拍說謝謝。Lead everyone in a 5-minute warm-up before you play; after the game, tap paddles with every player and say thanks.
1
第一關Level 1

握好球拍Hold the paddle

像跟球拍握手一樣，輕輕握住。Shake hands with the paddle—and hold it gently.
①拍面立起來，手像要跟它握手，自然握住拍柄。這叫「東方式握法」，張教練說新手先從這個開始。Stand the paddle on its edge and grip the handle like a handshake. This is the Eastern grip—Coach Chang says beginners start here.
②不要握太緊！握太緊手會變硬，球就控制不好。Don't squeeze! A tight grip makes your arm stiff and the ball hard to control.
③握法沒有標準答案，能把球穩穩打好最重要。There's no single right grip—hitting the ball steadily is what matters.
小挑戰Challenge
拍面朝上，把球往上輕輕顛，連續 10 下不落地。Paddle face up, bounce the ball gently upward 10 times in a row without dropping it.

第二關Level 2

發球The serve

站在底線後面，由下往上，發到斜對面。Stand behind the back line, swing low to high, serve to the box diagonally across.
①站在底線後面，腳不要踩到線。Stand behind the baseline—don't step on the line.
②手由下往上揮，在腰的下面打到球。也可以讓球從手上自然掉下來，彈一下再打（不能用丟的）。Swing from low to high and hit the ball below your waist. Or let the ball drop from your hand, bounce once, then hit it (just drop it—no throwing).
③球要飛過網子，落在斜對面的發球區。每個人只有一次機會。The ball must clear the net and land in the service box diagonally across. You get one try.

⚠️ 球落在對面的「廚房」裡，或壓到廚房的線，都不算。發球擦到網、但還是落在對的發球區，就繼續打！⚠️ A serve landing in the kitchen—or on the kitchen line—doesn't count. If it clips the net but still lands in the right box, keep playing!

小挑戰Challenge
發 10 顆球，數數看有幾顆落進斜對面的發球區。下次試著多進一顆！Serve 10 balls and count how many land in the diagonal box. Next time, try to beat your score by one!

第三關Level 3

記住：彈兩次Remember: two bounces

前兩球都要等它落地彈起來，才可以打。The first two shots must bounce before anyone hits them.
① 發球① Serve對方要等球彈一次They wait for one bounce
② 回球② Return發球的這一隊也要等球彈一次The serving team waits for one bounce too
③ 之後③ After that可以在空中直接打（截擊），也可以等彈起來再打Hit it out of the air (a volley) or after a bounce—your choice
小挑戰Challenge
和夥伴玩「彈彈接力」：每一球都等它落地彈起再打，連續來回 10 拍不失誤。Play "bounce relay" with a partner: let every ball bounce before you hit it, and keep 10 shots going without a miss.
4
第四關Level 4

廚房與丁克球The kitchen and the dink

站在廚房裡，不可以打空中球。In the kitchen, no hitting the ball out of the air.
①「廚房」是網子兩邊、各大約 2 公尺的區域，連線也算在裡面。The "kitchen" is the area about 2 meters (7 feet) from the net on each side—the lines count as part of it.
②腳踩在廚房裡（或踩到線）時，不能打還沒落地的球。打完空中球後腳滑進去，也不行。With a foot in the kitchen (or on its line), you can't hit a ball before it bounces. Stumbling in right after a volley doesn't count either.
③「丁克球」就是輕輕一推，讓球落進對方的廚房。穩穩打、不要急，等對方打出太高的球再進攻。A "dink" is a soft push that lands in the other team's kitchen. Stay steady, be patient, and attack when they send one up too high.
小挑戰Challenge
兩個人都站在廚房線外面，輕輕把球推進對方的廚房，連續 10 下不失誤。Both players stand just outside the kitchen line and softly push the ball into each other's kitchen—10 in a row without a miss.
看丁克示範Watch: dinks
5
第五關Level 5

學會報分Call the score

只有發球的那一隊可以得分。打到 11 分、還要多對方 2 分才贏。Only the serving team can score. First to 11—and you must be 2 points ahead to win.
①雙打發球前要說三個數字：我們的分數、對方的分數、我是第幾號發球員（1 或 2）。Before serving in doubles, say three numbers: our score, their score, and whether you're server 1 or 2.
②比賽一開始要說「0-0-2」：第一輪只有一個人發球，失誤就換對方發。A game starts with "0-0-2": the first turn has only one server, and a miss passes the serve to the other team.
③發球的隊伍贏了這一球，就加 1 分，發球的人和隊友換邊，繼續發。When the serving team wins the rally, they get 1 point; the server swaps sides with their partner and serves again.

用下面的報分模擬器闖關：每按一次按鈕前，先大聲猜猜看新的比分是多少，再看看答對了沒！Use the score trainer below: before each tap, guess the new score out loud—then check if you got it right!
